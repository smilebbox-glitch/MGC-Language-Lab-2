Important staged-cleanup note
-----------------------------
Historical renderer declarations are still physically present in `static/app.js` in v6.0.13.
They are no longer exposed through the compatibility bridge and are set to `undefined` at runtime after their modular replacement owners have loaded.
Physical source deletion is intentionally deferred until learning/practice and remaining shared lifecycle primitives are migrated.
