MGC Languages v5.9.7 — User Login & Department Onboarding

Purpose:
- make tomorrow's local-office pilot usable by real employees;
- require department selection in browser auth;
- persist department in the existing User model;
- provide a stable configurable admin account for the LAN profile.

Browser auth:
- registration: display name + username + department + password;
- login: username + department + password;
- ordinary-user department mismatch -> 401;
- admin department is not treated as an authentication secret.

LAN admin:
- username: admin;
- department: Администрация;
- password: supplied locally at first launch or in .env.lan;
- MGC_ADMIN_SYNC_CREDENTIALS=true in LAN profile;
- sync occurs after migrations and before Uvicorn workers start;
- production requires explicit ALLOW_ADMIN_CREDENTIAL_RESET=true.
