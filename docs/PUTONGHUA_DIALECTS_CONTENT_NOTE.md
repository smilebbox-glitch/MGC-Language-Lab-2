# Putonghua and Chinese regional varieties — content note v5.3

## Why this exists
Russian-speaking beginners often hear two apparently conflicting statements:
1. “Learn Mandarin / Putonghua.”
2. “China has many dialects and people may speak very differently.”

The module resolves this without turning the course into a linguistics textbook.

## Core teaching statement
**普通话 (Putonghua)** is taught as the practical nationwide standard for cross-regional communication. The learner is not told that local varieties are “bad Chinese”. They are normal regional language varieties and part of China's linguistic diversity.

The official Ministry of Education overview describes Putonghua as the standard variety of modern Chinese, with standard pronunciation based on Beijing pronunciation, Northern speech as the base dialect, and normative grammar based on exemplary modern vernacular writing.

## “How many dialects?”
The UI deliberately does **not** claim that China has exactly ten dialects. There is no single useful exact count of local speech varieties. The beginner-friendly statement is:

> The Ministry of Education overview commonly groups Chinese dialects into **10 major groups**, with many local varieties inside them.

The ten groups shown are:
- 官话 — Mandarin / Guanhua
- 晋语 — Jin
- 吴语 — Wu
- 闽语 — Min
- 客家话 — Hakka
- 粤语 — Yue
- 湘语 — Xiang
- 赣语 — Gan
- 徽语 — Hui
- 平话土话 — Pinghua / Tuhua

## Practical examples used in the UI
- 四川话 is presented as a Southwestern Mandarin variety (西南官话).
- Cantonese is presented as a well-known Yue variety.
- Shanghainese is presented as a well-known Wu variety.

These examples are orientation aids, not a complete dialectology map.

## Audio boundary
The pilot does **not** synthesize dialect pronunciation. The local TTS voice is used only for standard Mandarin and English. Dialect audio would require validated native/approved voice sources; generating “Cantonese” or “Shanghainese” with a Mandarin voice would teach the wrong sound system.

## Workplace message
The user learns one useful standard-language request:

`请说普通话，可以吗？`

`qǐng shuō pǔtōnghuà, kěyǐ ma?`

“Можно, пожалуйста, говорить на Путунхуа?”

## Primary references used for content review
1. Ministry of Education of the PRC, **中国语言文字概况（2021年版）**, published 2021-08-27. It defines the standard-language basis and lists the ten major dialect groups.
2. Ministry of Education of the PRC, **中华人民共和国国家通用语言文字法**, revised 2025-12-27 and effective 2026-01-01. It defines Putonghua and standardized Chinese characters as the national standard spoken and written language.
3. National standard **GB/T 46236.1-2025 — 中国语言资源保护调查规范 第1部分：汉语方言**, effective 2026-03-01, for current Chinese language-resource/dialect survey standardization context.

## Governance
Before broad corporate rollout, a Language Expert should review regional examples and wording. The learning app should not use dialect/variety labels as employee competence scores or cultural stereotypes.
